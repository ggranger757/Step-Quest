Country -> National bird mapping used:
Canada (CAN) -> Common Loon (de facto, on $1 coin "loonie"; also 2016 national bird contest winner Canada Jay)
United Kingdom (GBR) -> European Robin
Australia (AUS) -> Emu
Japan (JPN) -> Green Pheasant
Mexico (MEX) -> Golden Eagle
France (FRA) -> Gallic Rooster
Germany (DEU) -> German Federal Eagle (Golden Eagle / Bundesadler)
India (IND) -> Indian Peafowl (Peacock)
Brazil (BRA) -> Rufous-bellied Thrush
China (CHN) -> Red-crowned Crane
South Africa (ZAF) -> Blue Crane
New Zealand (NZL) -> Kiwi (North Island brown kiwi)
Spain (ESP) -> Spanish Imperial Eagle
Italy (ITA) -> Italian Sparrow
Argentina (ARG) -> Rufous Hornero
Colombia (COL) -> Andean Condor
Chile (CHL) -> Andean Condor
Peru (PER) -> Andean Cock-of-the-rock
Kenya (KEN) -> Lilac-breasted Roller
Thailand (THA) -> Siamese Fireback
Egypt (EGY) -> Steppe Eagle
Philippines (PHL) -> Philippine Eagle
Norway (NOR) -> White-throated Dipper
Sweden (SWE) -> Common Blackbird (voted 1962 and 2015)
Finland (FIN) -> Whooper Swan
Denmark (DNK) -> Mute Swan
Netherlands (NLD) -> Black-tailed Godwit
Ireland (IRL) -> Northern Lapwing
Greece (GRC) -> Little Owl (sacred to Athena; on 1-euro coin)
Poland (POL) -> White-tailed Eagle (on coat of arms since 1295)
Indonesia (IDN) -> Javan Hawk-Eagle
Nigeria (NGA) -> Black Crowned Crane
South Korea (KOR) -> Korean Magpie
Ukraine (UKR) -> White Stork
Israel (ISR) -> Eurasian Hoopoe (2008 public vote)
Pakistan (PAK) -> Chukar Partridge
Singapore (SGP) -> Crimson Sunbird
United States (USA) -> Bald Eagle (officially designated national bird Dec 24, 2024 by US legislation)
Iceland (ISL) -> Gyrfalcon
Belgium (BEL) -> Common Kestrel
Czech Republic (CZE) -> Common Kingfisher
Austria (AUT) -> Barn Swallow
Hungary (HUN) -> Saker Falcon (sacred Turul of Hungarian myth)
Taiwan (TWN) -> Taiwan Blue Magpie
Malaysia (MYS) -> Rhinoceros Hornbill
Vietnam (VNM) -> Red-whiskered Bulbul
Bangladesh (BGD) -> Oriental Magpie-Robin
UAE (ARE) -> Saker Falcon
Uganda (UGA) -> Grey Crowned Crane
Costa Rica (CRI) -> Clay-colored Thrush
Jamaica (JAM) -> Red-billed Streamertail (Doctor Bird)
Guatemala (GTM) -> Resplendent Quetzal
